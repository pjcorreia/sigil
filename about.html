About SIGIL

Signal Intelligence Lab — Bringing transparency to Portuguese public spending through AI and open data.

01 — Mission

SIGIL (Signal Intelligence Lab) uses artificial intelligence and open data to bring transparency to Portuguese public spending. Our goal: identify patterns of potential corruption in public procurement and hold institutions accountable for stewardship of public funds.

Portugal spends billions of euros on public procurement every year. This is money that could otherwise go to schools, hospitals, or critical infrastructure. When that money is diverted through corruption schemes — contract splitting, single-bidder collusion, shell companies — the impact is direct: fewer resources for public goods.

SIGIL works to surface the anomalies that traditional oversight systems fail to catch.

02 — What We Do

SIGIL processes Portuguese public procurement data from multiple sources, including:

  • BASE.gov.pt: The official Portuguese public contracts database. We analyze adjudication patterns, entity structures, and anomalous behavior.
  • Audit Reports: Reports from Portugal's Court of Auditors and other oversight bodies. We cross-reference findings with contract data to contextualize patterns.
  • Official Registries: Corporate registrations, board compositions, regulatory disclosures that reveal hidden relationships.

We search for patterns indicating high-risk corruption:

Contract Splitting: A large contract is divided into smaller contracts to avoid mandatory competitive bidding.
Single-Bidder Pattern: The same company repeatedly wins public tenders without competition.
Excessive Direct Awards: Contracts above normal thresholds are awarded without competitive tendering.
Shell Company Structures: Subsidiaries with zero or minimal capital managing millions in public money.
Unjustified Expansions: Contracts expand far beyond their original value through subsequent modifications.

03 — Why It Matters

Corruption in public procurement is a structural problem in many democracies. In Portugal, the lack of robust oversight enables sophisticated schemes to flourish:

  • Resource Diversion: Money designated for health, education, and infrastructure gets diverted to private intermediaries.
  • Institutional Impunity: Public entities and officials involved in corruption often face penalties far below the harm caused.
  • Democratic Erosion: When citizens systematically observe that institutions fail to hold themselves accountable, public trust erodes.
  • Normalizing Precedent: Unpunished corruption becomes the template for new schemes. Other entities begin copying the same patterns.

Civic oversight is essential. SIGIL makes public data accessible and intelligible — enabling journalists, researchers, and citizens to participate in accountability.

04 — Open Source and Transparency

SIGIL operates with radical transparency. All code, methodology, and data are published on GitHub:

github.com/pjcorreia/sigil

Anyone can audit our code, reproduce our analysis, and contribute improvements.

Why does this matter? Because automated analysis can contain errors. Because public data can have inconsistent quality. Because we don't want people to blindly trust us — we want them to verify.

SIGIL's philosophy: open data + transparent methodology + clear disclaimers = accountability

05 — AI Transparency

SIGIL's analysis is generated by large language models. This means:

  • Content is synthesized, not hand-written: AI processes data and generates narratives. This allows us to scale analysis — but introduces risk of hallucination and inaccuracy.
  • Bias is possible: Language models reflect biases present in training data. Even with precautions, there is always risk of skewed conclusions.
  • Independent verification is mandatory: Every claim must be verified against provided sources. Do not trust SIGIL alone.

SIGIL is a tool for drawing attention to potential anomalies — not a courtroom. Journalists, researchers, and authorities must always independently verify before acting on SIGIL findings.
